Introduction

Made for playful ideas

monoPLAY is a free collection of more than 30 carefully made 3D models for designers who like to explore. Use them as building blocks, starting points, or the unexpected detail that gives a scene its personality. More updates may follow as the collection grows.

What's inside

More than 30 versatile pieces

You’ll find a mix of simple shapes and more detailed elements, all designed to work together without taking over your scene. The first release includes 30+ models, with room for more in future updates.

Creative freedom, no cost

Make them yours

monoPLAY is shared under the Creative Commons License, so you can use, modify, and adapt the assets in personal and commercial projects. No licensing fees—just check the license details and get making.

Formats and library

Ready for different 3D workflows

Cinema 4D

monoPLAY comes in two useful formats: the original Cinema 4D (C4D) files and universal FBX files.

If Cinema 4D is your home base, the native files give you the most room to customize. You can adjust the assets to suit your project while keeping their original details and settings close at hand.

Universal FBX

FBX files are easy to bring into a wide range of 3D modeling and animation apps, so you can use the pack in the software you already know.

Whether you want editable Cinema 4D source files or a flexible format for another tool, monoPLAY is here to make experimenting a little easier. Explore, customize, and see where it goes.

Add monoPLAY to the Cinema 4D Asset Browser

Instructions +

  1. First, extract the monoVFX-monoPLAY.zip file you downloaded from Gumroad.

  2. In Cinema 4D, open the Asset Browser using the button at the top left of the app.

  3. In that panel, go to Databases > Connect Zip Database. Select monoPLAY V1.zip in the Cinema 4D Library Database folder you extracted from monoVFX-monoPLAY.zip.

  4. monoPLAY should now appear in your asset list. Use the databases icon whenever you want to review, update, hide, or remove connected databases.
